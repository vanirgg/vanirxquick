About

Vanir and Quick esports merged in 2025 to form Vanir Quick, a new esports organization focused on competitive gaming and community engagement.
With a strong foundation in both competitive play and community, Vanir Quick aims to build a vibrant community of gamers and fans around the world.
Our mission is to provide top-tier esports entertainment while fostering a positive and inclusive environment for players and fans alike.

Operations Portal

Our in-house operations portal is a powerful tool designed to streamline the operations of our esports organization.
It provides a centralized platform for managing the organization as a whole, including team rosters, scheduling, tracking different kinds of metrics and facilitating communication between players, coaches, and staff.
With an intuitive interface and robust features, our operations portal helps us stay organized and efficient as we navigate the competitive esports landscape and the day to day.
Be sure to stay tuned and check out our portal when we launch!
